Sandbox Testing: from registration to results

Five steps between opening an account and getting a report you can act on.

  1. Register on the platform. Create your organization profile and verify your account.
  2. Choose a Sandbox. Pick Functionality Sandbox or Security Sandbox.
  3. Fill in device details. Submit specifications, protocols and target standards.
  4. Ship your device. Send it to the designated testing location.
  5. Track results live. Watch progress and download the final report.

Frequently asked questions

What is IoT Sandbox?

IoT Sandbox is C-DAC Bangalore’s testing ecosystem where IoT devices are registered, validated for functionality and assessed for security before they go into the field.

Which verticals offer device testing and reports?

Only Functionality Sandbox and Security Sandbox. Test requests, device shipment tracking and reports are managed through your dashboard.

What are Solution Providers on this portal?

Device Security Solution and Network Security Solution showcase products and services of solution providers. The portal does not test devices or generate reports for these two verticals.

What types of devices can be tested?

Security Sandbox supports Consumer IoT, Automotive Electronics, Industrial Control and Medical Devices. See the Security Sandbox page.

Security Sandbox

Assess security posture and identify vulnerabilities in a safe, isolated environment.

Supported device types

Choose your device class when raising a request. Procedures map to these standards.

Consumer IoT

Smart appliances, gadgets, utilities.

Standards

  • ETSI EN 303 645
  • NISTIR 8425
  • OWASP ISVS
  • STQC essential requirements

Automotive Electronics

ECUs, TCUs, in-vehicle systems, V2X.

Standards & protocols

  • AIS 189/190, ISO/SAE 21434
  • CAN, LIN, FlexRay, Auto-Ethernet
  • UDS, OBD2, XCP

Industrial Control

PLCs, sensors, actuators, SCADA, HMI, RTU, IED.

Standards & protocols

  • IEC 62443 (incl. 4-2 component level)
  • Profinet, Modbus, OPC UA, DLMS, EtherCAT

Medical Devices

Consumer medical and hospital grade.

Standards & protocols

  • IEC 60601-4-5
  • HL7, DICOM, FHIR

What you receive

  • Live test status on your dashboard
  • Downloadable test report
