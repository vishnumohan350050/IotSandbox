FAQs

Find your answers

What is IoT Sandbox?

IoT Sandbox is C-DAC Bengaluru's testing ecosystem where IoT devices are registered, validated for functionality and assessed for security before they go into the field.

What types of devices can be tested in the IoT Sandbox?

Connected devices across consumer, industrial and automotive IoT classes — anything communicating over the protocols supported by our four testing verticals.

What kind of testing is supported in the IoT Sandbox?

Functionality validation, security and penetration testing, network security assessment, and device/firmware-level security checks.

Can the sandbox simulate real-world IoT scenarios?

Yes — the sandbox is built to reproduce real network conditions, protocol interactions and load patterns your device will meet after deployment.

Is the sandbox compliant with industry standards?

Testing is mapped against recognized global IoT security and interoperability standards, so results translate directly into compliance evidence.

Can I test Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) or secure boot mechanisms in the sandbox?

Yes — PQC readiness and secure boot verification are covered under the Device Security vertical.

How are DUTs integrated with the sandbox?

Devices under test are registered on the portal, shipped to the assigned facility, and connected through the testing bay matching their vertical.

Who can benefit from an IoT Sandbox?

Device manufacturers, integrators and research teams who need independent functionality and security validation before deployment.

Can AI/ML models be tested within the sandbox?

On-device AI/ML components can be evaluated as part of functionality and security testing where they affect device behaviour.
